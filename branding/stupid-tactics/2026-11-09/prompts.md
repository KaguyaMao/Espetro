## 1. Main logo / generation

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original, professionally designed logo for a Minecraft combined-arms tactical server. The complete brand name is Stupid Tactics Minecraft. Look to the genre of Broken Arrow and Squad for bold military typographic confidence and restrained equipment silhouettes, but design a distinct original identity.
Asset: final isolated logo on a genuinely transparent background, wide landscape composition, tightly balanced with generous transparent safety margins. Not a presentation board.
Text must read EXACTLY, in these three centered rows:
"STUPID"
"TACTICS"
"MINECRAFT"
STUPID and TACTICS together form the dominant main title, two equal-width rows in huge, custom condensed heavy uppercase military sans lettering. Crisp flat faces, squared corners, a few controlled stencil cuts; very subtle sparse edge wear at most, never damage legibility. MINECRAFT is the subordinate subtitle, about 18–22 percent the title cap height, widely spaced simple uppercase lettering underneath with short balanced rules at either side. Do not imitate Minecraft's official block letter logo.
A clean, low-profile modern main-battle-tank side silhouette sits along the top edge of the wordmark. It should have a long left-pointing gun barrel, angular low turret with block-built stepped armor, squat tracked hull and 6 large readable wheels; the geometry should evoke block-based DragonRise Reforge Minecraft armored vehicles rather than realistic photographic detail. Simplify all tiny antennas and armor details. Tank is a secondary graphic, about 20 percent of the total logo height; lettering remains dominant.
Color: warm off-white main lettering and subtitle, signal-yellow tank and one small disciplined yellow alignment accent, fine near-black edge definition so the logo can also be recognized against a pale background. Flat solid inks. Integrate the tank and type into one compact silhouette, precise optical spacing, strong negative space, readable at thumbnail scale.
No other text, no slogans, no dates, no mockup, no background rectangle, no landscape, no shadows cast on a background, no bevels, no metallic 3D extrusion, no gradients, no glow, no lens flare, no camouflage pattern, no flags, no MOC-T text, no soldier crowd, no shield, no skull, no watermark. All transparent areas, including counters and spaces around the tank, must remain genuinely transparent.
